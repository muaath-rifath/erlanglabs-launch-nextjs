# Product Features & Workflow Guide

## Overview
Welcome to our AI Outbound Calling Platform. This product is designed to completely automate your outreach, lead qualification, and appointment scheduling through intelligent, conversational AI agents. By streamlining the entire process—from phone number acquisition to CRM and calendar integrations—our platform allows you to launch dynamic, data-driven voice campaigns in just a few clicks.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Account & Agent Management
* **Account Creation**: Securely sign up and manage your organization's workspace.
* **AI Agent Creation**: Design and customize your dedicated AI agents. You can configure their personas and behaviors to perfectly align with your brand's voice and outreach goals.
* **Knowledge Base (KB) Association**: Equip your AI agents with specific Knowledge Bases. This ensures your agents have all the contextual facts, product details, and FAQs required to answer questions accurately and speak authoritatively during live calls.

### 2. Telephony & Compliance
* **KYC Verification**: Complete a streamlined Know Your Customer (KYC) process to ensure regulatory compliance and secure your telephony resources.
* **DID (Direct Inward Dialing) Purchasing**: Once verified, you can browse and purchase dedicated phone numbers (DIDs) directly within the platform. These numbers act as the caller ID for your automated outbound campaigns.

### 3. Campaign Creation & Lead Management
* **Campaign Builder**: An intuitive, centralized interface to construct and manage your outreach campaigns. To set up a campaign, you simply select your preferred Knowledge Base, your purchased DID, and your assigned AI agent.
* **Flexible Lead Sourcing**: Import your list of targets or leads using multiple convenient methods:
  * **Spreadsheet Upload**: Easily upload lead lists via spreadsheets.
  * **CRM Integrations**: Seamlessly sync leads directly from your existing workflows using our native, one-click integrations with **Zoho CRM** and **HubSpot**.
* **Dynamic Data Collection**: Customize your campaign by defining specific data "columns." These are the exact pieces of information you want the AI agent to extract from the prospect during the call (e.g., budget, timeline, level of interest). The AI will naturally steer the conversation to gather this critical data.

### 4. Advanced Integrations
* **Appointment Scheduling**: We offer native integration with **cal.com**. If a lead is qualified and expresses interest, the AI agent can autonomously schedule a follow-up appointment or demo directly onto your calendar in real-time.

### 5. Automated Execution
* **One-Click Launch**: Once your campaign is fully configured (Agent, KB, DID, Leads, and Data Columns), simply click the **"Play"** button on the campaign dashboard.
* **Autonomous Outbound Calling**: The platform immediately begins dialing the numbers on your list. Upon connection, the AI speaks directly to the users, holding natural, human-like conversations.
* **Intelligent Logging**: As the conversation progresses, the AI actively listens, responds contextually based on its KB, schedules appointments via cal.com, and automatically populates your requested data columns with the information collected from the lead.

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Sign Up**: Create your user account and log into the main dashboard.
2. **Build Your Agent**: Create your AI agent and attach the relevant Knowledge Bases.
3. **Get Your Number**: Complete the quick KYC process and buy a DID number.
4. **Setup Your Campaign**: 
   - Select your active Knowledge Bases, your DID, and your AI Agent.
   - Import your leads via a spreadsheet upload, or pull them in through Zoho CRM or HubSpot.
   - Create custom data columns for the specific information you want the agent to collect.
   - Connect your cal.com account for seamless appointment booking.
5. **Launch**: Click "Play" on the campaign. The system will automatically handle the calling, speak to your targets, gather the required information, and book meetings on your behalf.
